The user prefers short answers that lead with the result, no preamble and no recap of what was
just done. Code, identifiers and commit messages are in English; conversation can be in any
language they start with.

**Why:** they read the answer between other tasks and skim; filler costs them attention.
**How to apply:** one or two sentences for a result, a list only when there are several parallel items.
